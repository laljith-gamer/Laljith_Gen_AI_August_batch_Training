# Data Analyst Career Roadmap & Skill Guide

## Core Responsibilities
A Data Analyst inspects, cleans, transforms, and models data to discover actionable business insights, construct executive dashboards, and support operational decision-making.

## Primary Technical Skills
1. Structured Query Language (SQL): Advanced querying, multi-table JOINs, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), window functions (ROW_NUMBER, RANK, LAG/LEAD), aggregation, and performance query tuning.
2. Business Intelligence & Dashboarding: Power BI, Tableau, Looker. Designing star-schema data models, writing DAX measures (in Power BI) or calculated fields (in Tableau).
3. Spreadsheet Modeling: Microsoft Excel (VLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, Pivot Tables, Power Query, scenario analysis).
4. Python for Analytics: Pandas (data wrangling), NumPy (numerical calculations), Matplotlib and Seaborn (exploratory visualization).
5. Statistical Foundations: Hypothesis testing, p-values, A/B testing, normal distribution, correlation vs causation, regression analysis.

## Recommended Transition Pathway
For candidates transitioning into Data Analytics from general programming, business operations, or QA:
- Step 1: Master SQL by solving real business questions on sample schemas (retail, finance, healthcare).
- Step 2: Build 2-3 portfolio dashboard projects in Tableau or Power BI demonstrating storytelling with data.
- Step 3: Learn basic Python data manipulation with Pandas to automate repetitive reporting workflows.
- Step 4: Add clear business impact metrics to resume bullets (e.g. "Identified churn patterns leading to 12% retention improvement").
